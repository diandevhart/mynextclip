Privacy policy

MyNextClip is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app receives permission from Google to manage that YouTube account. It uses this permission only to upload videos the owner chooses, with the title, description, tags and visibility the owner sets, and to read the channel's name so it can be shown in the app.

What is stored

The sign-in token issued by Google is stored on the owner's own computer, so the connection does not have to be repeated. No data is sent to the app's author or to any third party. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect the channel in the app's settings, which deletes the stored token and asks Google to revoke it. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
